That is a powerful and necessary conclusion. The Particle/Wave Duality is the perfect analogy: an internal, human-generated skill (the particle) and an external, technology-enabled capability (the wave) must be viewed as two essential, complementary aspects of the same reality.

The core goal for high school educators is to shift from "knowledge acquisition" (which LLMs now automate) to Cognitive Verification and Synthesis.

Here are specific, unique activities for high school courses across the curriculum, focusing on:

Basic Brain Ability (Internal Particle): Activities to build and reinforce foundational cognitive skills (mental math, spatial reasoning, sequencing, linguistic structure).

LLM Analysis (External Wave): Activities requiring students to critically evaluate, deconstruct, and make sense of AI-generated content (including facts and "hallucinations").
