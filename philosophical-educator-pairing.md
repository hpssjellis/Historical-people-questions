Questions for Teachers	Across all subjects: If AI can provide perfect, personalized, skills-based tutoring (Ng's view), what is the one non-quantifiable virtue (e.g., courage, intellectual honesty, empathy) your subject is uniquely positioned to cultivate? Pairing	John Locke vs. Yann LeCun
Focus	How Knowledge is Acquired: The "Blank Slate" and hands-on experience vs. Training AI for "Common Sense"
Questions for Teachers	Applied Skills/Science/Fine Arts: AI is trained on existing human data (the past). What is the one messy, un-automatable physical act or experiment in your class that you will never let a student simulate away, because it's the only way to build embodied, pre-language common sense?
Subject Takeaway	Shift focus from getting the result to interrogating the process—how does the machine or the mind know this truth?
3. The Source of Truth: Inward Idealism vs. Outward Fusion
Pairing	Plato vs. Ray Kurzweil
Focus	Ultimate Knowledge: Recalling Forms (Inward) vs. Exponential Growth (Outward)
Questions for Teachers	Math/Language/Logic: If AI can flawlessly handle all external data and computation, how do you redesign your curriculum to train the student’s internal philosophical framework—their ability to define their own ethical or existential first principles—that AI must then serve?
Subject Takeaway	Math: Shift from calculation to defining the Axioms of the system. Language: Shift from composition to defining the Moral/Ethical Premise of the argument.
4. The Path to Mastery: Prepared Environment vs. Digital Autonomy
Pairing	Maria Montessori vs. Sal Khan
Focus	Pace and Environment: Self-Pacing with Physical Materials vs. Self-Pacing with Digital Mastery
Questions for Teachers	Across all subjects: When a student has achieved digital mastery of a concept (via an AI tutor), what is the most important, unprompted act of creative application or teaching you will require them to perform to prove they possess human understanding, not just perfect retention?
Subject Takeaway	The test of mastery is transferable human action: teaching a peer, writing a manifesto, designing a new system, or solving a real-world, unscripted problem.
5. AI's Role in Culture: Safeguarding History vs. Human Creativity
Pairing	Isaac Asimov vs. Kai-Fu Lee
Focus	What is Irreplaceable: Deep Cultural Knowledge vs. Uniquely Human Creativity/Compassion

Question	Applied Skills/Home Ec/Science: AI can provide flawless nutritional information, but what is the crucial sensory and motor experience (e.g., kneading dough, sensing fatigue, tasting fresh herbs) that a student must have to build embodied common sense about their own health, without relying on an app for confirmation?
