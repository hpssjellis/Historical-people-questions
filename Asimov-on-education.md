This would result in a society of enforced leisure where most people would simply be machine-tenders, without any meaningful, challenging, or creative work to occupy their minds.

The Link to Modern Social Media Use:

Providing Endless, Low-Effort Distraction: Social media feeds are an inexhaustible source of stimulation that requires minimal cognitive effort, serving as a perfect anesthetic for the boredom Asimov predicted.

Replacing Creative Work with Passive Consumption: The "lucky few who can be involved in creative work of any sort will be the true elite," he wrote. Social media encourages consumption and reaction over deep, creative production, exactly what Asimov warned would happen when people no longer needed to think to survive.

He passionately argued that the computer's greatest educational power was its ability to inspire and satisfy curiosity.
