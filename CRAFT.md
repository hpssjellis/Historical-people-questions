The most well-known and commonly used cognitive screening test for older adults is the Mini-Mental State Examination (MMSE).

However, there are several other widely used screening tests as well, including:

Montreal Cognitive Assessment (MoCA): Often considered more sensitive than the MMSE for detecting Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI).

Mini-Cog: A very quick, three-minute test that combines a recall test and a clock drawing test.

Saint Louis University Mental Status (SLUMS) Examination: An alternative to the MMSE and MoCA, also designed to detect both dementia and MCI.

Self-Administered Gerocognitive Exam (SAGE): A self-administered test that can be taken at home.

The choice of which test to use often depends on the setting (e.g., primary care, specialist clinic), the patient's education level, and the time available.
